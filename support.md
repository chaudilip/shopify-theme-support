# Larder support

Help with the Larder theme: setting it up, a setting that does not behave as
the guide describes, or something that looks wrong on your store.

## Before you write

Most questions are answered in one of these:

- **[Setting up Larder](setup)** — the full setup guide. Its
  [Troubleshooting](setup#13-troubleshooting) section covers the questions asked
  most often.
- **[Custom data](custom-data)** — nutrition panels, ingredients, dietary
  badges and the other product details that use Shopify metafields.

## Contact

**Open an issue:** [github.com/chaudilip/shopify-theme-support/issues](https://github.com/chaudilip/shopify-theme-support/issues)

Please include:

- your store URL
- the theme version (this documentation covers Larder **1.1.0**)
- the page where the problem shows, and a screenshot
- what you expected to happen, and what happened instead

Issues are answered in the order they arrive.

## What support covers

- Help using the theme's sections, blocks and settings
- Faults in the theme itself

Changes to the theme's code, and help with apps from other developers, are
outside what theme support covers.
